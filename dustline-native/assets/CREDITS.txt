DUSTLINE / 沙境
Original playable map geometry, first-person weapon geometry, gameplay code and synthesized Web Audio effects.
Desert material atlas: AI-generated original asset created for this demo, September 2026.
Rifle, sidearm, gloves and tactical operator uniform: original Blender meshes and procedural PBR textures authored for this demo. Editable Blender projects are provided separately; reproducible scripts are in models/.
Tactical operator skeleton and Idle/Walk/Run animation: adapted from Mixamo's Soldier asset distributed in the official Three.js example asset collection. The original source GLB is retained for rebuilding the animation rig.
Source: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/examples/models/gltf/Soldier.glb
Reference: https://threejs.org/examples/webgl_animation_skinning_blending.html
